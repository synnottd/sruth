Sruth/sruh/ · Irish for stream

Stream once. Broadcast everywhere.

Sruth is a multi-destination live video relay. You send one stream to Sruth; Sruth forwards it to Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, Kick and any custom RTMP or SRT destination at the same time.

It is built for mobile and IRL streamers first. A phone on a cellular uplink physically cannot sustain three high-bitrate outputs at once — Sruth does the fan-out so your device doesn't have to.

What's different

  • SRT-native from day one. Modern, packet-loss-tolerant ingest for mobile and cellular connections. RTMP is supported too, but SRT is a first-class citizen, not an afterthought.
  • Honest, upfront pricing. No surprise bitrate caps, no "premium platform" surcharges, no fine print that makes your real bill something other than the one on the pricing page.
  • Mobile and IRL as the primary customer. The product is designed around the constraints of cellular uplinks, not retrofitted to them. Desktop streamers are welcome; they are not the wedge.
